Training Dataset

ASVspoof 2019 + FakeAVCeleb Combined Dataset

The model is trained on a combined dataset of ASVspoof 2019 (a standard benchmark for audio deepfake detection) and FakeAVCeleb (a large-scale dataset of real and fake celebrity voices). This gave us a total of 20,000+ audio samples with a balanced mix of real and fake clips, covering various synthesis methods and recording conditions.
